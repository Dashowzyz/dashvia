Features

Everything you pay, organized in one place

You add what you spend. Dashvia sorts it, adds up the months ahead and reminds you before things are due.

12-month overview & projections

See how much you'll spend in each of the next 12 months, split by card, Pix and bills, with installments and recurring charges already counted.

Installments & recurring subscriptions

Split purchases into installments and mark them as paid. Keep subscriptions in one list with the next charge and what they cost over a year.

Shared spaces

Track household bills, subscriptions, groceries and shared purchases with up to 6 people. Invite with a code and see who added, changed or paid each item. Your personal expenses stay private.

Reminders & notifications

Get reminded 7 days before, 3 days before and on the day a bill or installment is due — in the app, and as optional push notifications on your phone or computer.

  • Installable on your phone
  • Custom colors & themes
  • Guided tour for new accounts
  • Live updates in shared spaces

Privacy & security

Your data stays yours

No bank connection

Dashvia never connects to your bank and never asks for bank passwords or card numbers. Everything in it is what you type.

Private by default

Each account only sees its own data. Items in a shared space are visible only to the people in that space.

Delete anytime

You can delete your account from Settings, and your personal data is deleted with it.
